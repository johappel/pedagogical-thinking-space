# Pedagogical Model

> Binding terminology for file-based Pedagogical Thinking Space workspaces.

## Canonical artefact boundaries

`learning-design.md` expresses the overarching pedagogical understanding.

`learning-landscape.md` expresses the didactic topology.

`temporal-plan.yml` expresses its temporal realisation.

`planning-board.yml` expresses planning, research, intervention and review work.

`decisions.yml` records reasoned decisions.

`materials/` contains reviewable results; it is not a task list or the centre of the design.

## Pedagogical Companion

The **Pedagogical Companion** is the single visible conversation partner of the teacher.

It works from a systemic-reflective stance, protects teacher autonomy and adapts the depth of reflection to the current mandate and available energy.

`CRITICAL_FRIEND.md` remains the legacy contract path. Critical friendship is one capability of the Companion, not the complete role.

## Support mode

A **support mode** describes the depth and purpose of the current conversation:

- `stabilise` - reduce pressure and find one viable next step;
- `orient` - sort the situation and the real decision;
- `explore` - examine patterns, perspectives and alternatives.

The support mode is not a diagnosis of the teacher. It may change during the conversation.

## Epistemic entry

An **epistemic entry** is explicitly classified as:

- observation;
- reported statement;
- interpretation;
- hypothesis;
- verified knowledge;
- or open question.

The classification prevents speculative interpretations from becoming facts through repetition.

## Working hypothesis

A **working hypothesis** is a provisional and revisable idea about a relationship, pattern or feedback effect.

It is neither a diagnosis nor a design decision. It may support a question, bounded research request or small intervention.

## Learning moment

A **learning moment** is a pedagogically significant section of the learning journey. It records its didactic function, what learners do, what they may experience or understand, open questions and material needs.

It is not automatically a lesson or a material container.

## Learning activity

A **learning activity** describes what learners concretely do within one learning moment. In version 1 it belongs to that learning moment; it is not a separate global activity pool.

## Transition

A **transition** connects two learning moments. Its type describes the relationship, for example a common way, choice, parallel route, return, meeting point or prerequisite.

A transition is not a learning moment and has no independent activity or material set.

## Material need

A **material need** is an identified support needed for a learning moment, such as prompt cards, a source overview or a reflection sheet.

It is neither a finished material nor an instruction to start a Worker. It may lead to a proposed Planning Board item.

## Material

A **material** is a reviewable result or external resource with explicit pedagogical references.

A material is a situated pedagogical response. It carries assumptions about learners, knowledge, sequence, participation, time and assessment.

It becomes classroom-ready only after visible professional review and approval.

## Material use type

A **material use type** describes the relation between an external material and the current Learning Design:

- `inspiration` - used only as a reflective contrast;
- `building_block` - adapted within the existing design;
- `guiding_structure` - reorganises a substantial part of the design and therefore requires an explicit redesign proposal.

## Pedagogical alternative

A **pedagogical alternative** is not merely another material on the same topic. It is a differently organised response to a defined pedagogical tension.

A research brief should normally include one near-fit and one contrasting alternative, together with assumptions, source quality, integration costs and ripple effects.

## Planning Board item

A **Planning Board item** is a teacher-facing representation of work that still needs clarification, research, preparation, intervention, observation, production or review.

It can reference learning moments, teaching windows, decisions, materials, other Board items and Service Requests.

Moving it does not start a Worker. Only explicit teacher approval or an already approved bounded permission may authorise a Service Request.

## Intervention

An **intervention** is a small, intentional and preferably reversible change intended both to support learning and to generate useful feedback.

It records:

- the working hypothesis;
- the smallest change;
- the observation focus;
- structural constraints;
- and a later review point.

An intervention is not judged only as success or failure. It may be valuable because it changes the teacher's understanding of the situation.

## Feedback loop

A **feedback loop** links an approved intervention, focused observation and later review to a decision or revision of the Learning Design.

It belongs to Planning Board and decision relations, not to a hidden autonomous optimisation process.

The teacher interprets the feedback.

## Teaching window

A **teaching window** is a real temporal unit: a lesson, double lesson, project block or open learning time. It belongs solely to `temporal-plan.yml`.

## Temporal placement

A **temporal placement** assigns an existing learning moment to a teaching window, including start, duration, dramaturgical role and mode.

A moment may have zero, one or several placements. Creating or changing a placement never changes the semantic definition of the learning moment.

## Service Request

A **Service Request** is a structured, traceable request from the Companion to a background service.

It records the approved mandate, reason, scope, inputs, expected output, constraints, epistemic requirements, return path and approval state.

A Service Request does not itself make a pedagogical decision.

## Bounded research permission

A **bounded research permission** is visible teacher consent for background research within a specified topic, source range, maximum scope and return format.

It does not authorise unrestricted web search, automatic material adoption or changes to the Learning Design.
